## Services That Do Not Accept Add-Ons

The following service categories are standalone treatments and are not compatible with the add-on menu:

- **Head Spa** — Scalp Massage, Scalp Treatment, Scalp Detox, Signature Head Spa
- **Waxing** — all face and body waxing services
